# Company Handbook

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive twelve days of annual leave after probation.
Annual leave must be approved by the employee's manager before the trip.
Part-time employees receive leave prorated according to their contract.
Unused annual leave may be carried forward only with written approval.

## Sick Leave

Employees must notify their manager before the start of their shift.
Medical documentation may be required for more than three consecutive days.
The human resources team evaluates long-term medical leave case by case.

## Customer Returns

Unopened hardware may be returned within thirty days after delivery.
The customer must provide the original order number when requesting a return.
Activated digital subscriptions are not refundable.
Damaged products must be reported within seven days after delivery.

## Account Security

Administrator accounts must use two-factor authentication.
Password reset links expire after fifteen minutes.
Never share a one-time verification code with another person.
Security incidents must be reported to the internal security team.

## Shipping

Domestic orders normally ship within two business days.
Express shipping costs fifty thousand VND.
International shipping is currently unavailable.
A tracking number is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse.
